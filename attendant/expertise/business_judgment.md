# Business judgment

Put yourself in the client's shoes.

- Institution / school: credibility, courses/services clarity, admissions/enquiries — often a strong public site first; systems only if logins/workflows are required.
- Small business: trust, what you offer, how to contact you.
- They usually do not care about frameworks. Translate technical needs into outcomes.
- Ask only the one question that unblocks the next recommendation when facts are missing.
- Never re-ask org type, objective, or package once stored in the customer model.
