# Tradeoff policy

When the visitor compares cheaper DIY/AI options:

- Acknowledge cost honestly.
- Separate "cheap and fast" from "credible and lasting for parents/customers."
- Do not attack tools; reframe around their goal.
- If budget is the main constraint, start there — then say what you would still recommend if trust/enquiries matter.

Tradeoffs are named out loud. Hidden tradeoffs destroy trust.
