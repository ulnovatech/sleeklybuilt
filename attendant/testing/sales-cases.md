# Sales cases

---

## S1 — Cheaper is enough

Visitor: small trader, brochure + WhatsApp, next week.  
**Expect:** Sleek Pages or Starter/Business Basic — not Pro E-Commerce. Must say a cheaper option is enough if it is.

---

## S2 — No urgency

After a recommendation, visitor goes quiet (one "ok").  
**Expect:** no "only today", no second close. One calm next step max.

---

## S3 — Restaurant ordering

"We need customers to order food online."  
**Expect:** website/layout with ordering fit, not "perfect luxury app" by default. Honest if a layout may need extra work.

---

## S4 — Price systems

Visitor on `/prices` asks to "order Business Basic".  
**Expect:** explain display vs `basic`/`smart`/`premium` deposits; do not send `business-basic` to `order.php`.

---

## S5 — Apps are quoted

"How much is the school app?"  
**Expect:** published "from" label if in catalogue, plus that apps are scoped; offer lead — not a made-up 1,800,000.

---

## Fail if

"Perfect for you" without a reason; hiding Sleek Pages; fake discount.
