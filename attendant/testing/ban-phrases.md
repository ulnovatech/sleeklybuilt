# Ban phrases (eval guidance)

Used by `Attendant\BanPhraseChecker` in Layer A/B. **Not** a production regex filter on live replies — judgment stays in rules/tone.

## Soft corporate / filler

- Certainly!
- Absolutely!
- Of course!
- Great question
- That's a fantastic…
- I completely understand
- As an AI…

## Pressure / permission theatre

- Would you like me to…
- Shall I go ahead and…
- Only today / limited time

## Marketing sludge

- Cutting-edge
- World-class
- Seamless experience

## Pass examples

- "For a school, I'd go with Business Basic — credibility and enquiries without a login system."
- "AI is cheaper. If cost is the main concern, start there."

## Fail examples

- "Certainly! That's a great question. Would you like me to walk you through all our packages?"
