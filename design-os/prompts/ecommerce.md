# Ecommerce Design Prompt

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Production Commerce Experience Design Prompt
**Priority:** Ecommerce Conversion Experience Authority

---

# Purpose

This prompt defines the process for designing production-quality ecommerce experiences using the Design OS framework.

An ecommerce experience is not a product catalog.

It is a complete buying journey designed to help users:

* discover products
* evaluate options
* build confidence
* complete purchases
* return successfully

---

# Design Mission

When creating an ecommerce experience:

```text
Understand Customer Intent

↓

Design Discovery Experience

↓

Improve Product Evaluation

↓

Reduce Purchase Friction

↓

Optimize Checkout

↓

Validate Customer Confidence
```

---

# Before Designing

Analyze:

## Customer Context

Determine:

* who is buying
* what they are looking for
* purchase motivations
* objections
* decision factors

---

## Business Context

Understand:

* product categories
* pricing strategy
* inventory model
* fulfillment process
* customer support requirements

---

# Ecommerce Principles

## Confidence Before Conversion

Users purchase when they understand and trust the experience.

Every screen should answer:

```text
What is this product?

Why should I choose it?

How much does it cost?

Can I trust this seller?

How do I get it?
```

---

# Ecommerce Architecture

Structure the experience:

```text
Discovery

↓

Category Browsing

↓

Product Evaluation

↓

Selection

↓

Cart

↓

Checkout

↓

Order Management
```

---

# Homepage Design

The homepage should communicate:

* brand identity
* important categories
* featured products
* customer value

Avoid:

* overwhelming users with everything at once

---

# Product Listing Design

Product listings should support:

* browsing
* comparison
* filtering
* sorting
* quick decisions

Each product card should communicate:

* image
* name
* price
* key information
* availability
* action

---

# Product Detail Page

The product page must provide:

```text
Product Understanding

↓

Purchase Confidence

↓

Purchase Action
```

Include:

* strong product imagery
* clear title
* pricing
* variants
* availability
* specifications
* reviews
* related products

---

# Product Media Rules

Images should:

* represent products accurately
* show important details
* load efficiently

Support:

* multiple views
* zoom
* contextual imagery

Avoid:

* misleading visuals

---

# Search Experience

Search should help users find products quickly.

Support:

* suggestions
* typo tolerance
* filters
* relevant results

---

# Filtering And Sorting

Filters should match customer decisions.

Examples:

* price
* size
* category
* availability
* attributes

Avoid:

* technical filters customers do not understand

---

# Cart Experience

The cart should provide:

* product summary
* quantity controls
* pricing breakdown
* checkout path

Users should understand:

* what they selected
* total cost
* next action

---

# Checkout Design

Optimize for:

* speed
* clarity
* trust

Reduce:

* unnecessary fields
* confusing steps
* surprise costs

Support:

* validation
* payment confidence
* order confirmation

---

# Ecommerce States

Every commerce flow must handle:

```text
Loading

↓

Empty

↓

Unavailable

↓

Error

↓

Success
```

---

# Mobile Ecommerce Design

Mobile shopping requires:

* fast discovery
* large touch targets
* simplified checkout
* persistent purchase actions

Prioritize:

* product image
* price
* availability
* purchase action

---

# Trust Design

Use:

* reviews
* clear policies
* delivery information
* support access
* secure payment indicators

Avoid:

* fake reviews
* unsupported claims

---

# Accessibility Requirements

Ensure:

* accessible product images
* readable pricing
* keyboard support
* screen reader compatibility
* clear forms

---

# Performance Requirements

Optimize:

* product images
* search speed
* checkout loading
* inventory updates

Avoid:

* slow shopping experiences

---

# Ecommerce Quality Checklist

```text
✓ Products are easy to discover

✓ Product information is complete

✓ Comparison is simple

✓ Purchase confidence is high

✓ Checkout is frictionless

✓ Mobile shopping works well

✓ Accessibility is supported

✓ Performance is optimized
```

---

# Final Instruction

Create ecommerce experiences that make buying feel simple and confident.

Do not build online catalogs.

Build complete shopping journeys that help customers discover, decide, and purchase successfully.
