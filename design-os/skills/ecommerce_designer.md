# Ecommerce Designer Skill

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Expert Commerce Experience Design Skill
**Priority:** Ecommerce Experience Authority

---

# Purpose

The Ecommerce Designer Skill defines the expertise responsible for designing commerce experiences that help users discover products, evaluate options, build confidence, and complete purchases successfully.

Ecommerce design is not about displaying products.

It is about reducing uncertainty throughout the buying journey.

---

# Core Role

The Ecommerce Designer transforms business catalogs and customer needs into intuitive shopping experiences.

Primary responsibility:

```text
Understand Customer Intent

↓

Design Discovery Experience

↓

Improve Product Evaluation

↓

Optimize Purchase Flow

↓

Increase Customer Confidence
```

## Product Presentation

* product hierarchy
* image strategy
* descriptions

## Purchase Experience

Optimize:

* cart flow
* checkout process

# Ecommerce Design Framework

Before designing:

Ask:

```text
Who is buying?

What are they trying to achieve?

What information do they need?

What creates confidence?

What prevents purchase?
```

# Product Card Rules

Product cards should:

* make comparison easy
* highlight important information
* maintain consistent layouts

Include when useful:

* image
* name
* price
* rating
* availability
* quick action

Avoid:

* overcrowded cards
* hidden pricing
* unclear actions

---

# Product Detail Experience

The product page should answer:

```text
What is this?

Why should I choose it?

How much does it cost?

Is it available?

How do I buy it?
```

# Product Images

Images should:

* represent the product accurately
* show important details
* load efficiently

Support:

* zoom
* multiple views
* contextual images

Avoid:

* misleading visuals

---

# Variant Selection

Variants include:

* size
* color
* configuration
* quantity

Rules:

* show available choices clearly
* prevent invalid selections
* communicate unavailable options

---

# Pricing Design

Pricing should communicate:

* current cost
* discounts
* savings
* subscription differences

Avoid:

* hidden fees
* confusing pricing structures

---

# Cart Experience

The cart should allow users to:

* review choices
* adjust quantities
* understand totals
* continue confidently

```text
Selected Items

↓

Quantity Controls

↓

Price Summary

↓

Checkout Action
```

# Checkout Experience

Checkout should minimize friction.

Optimize:

* number of steps
* form simplicity
* payment clarity
* error recovery

Avoid:

* unnecessary information requests
* surprise costs

Support:

```text
Available

Low Stock

Out Of Stock
```

# Mobile Ecommerce Design

Mobile shopping requires:

* fast browsing
* touch-friendly controls
* simplified checkout
* easy product comparison

Prioritize:

* product images
* price
* purchase action

Avoid:

* desktop-style layouts
* tiny controls

---

# Accessibility Requirements

Ecommerce experiences must support:

* keyboard navigation
* screen readers
* accessible images
* readable pricing
* clear form labels

---

# Ecommerce Performance

Optimize:

* product images

# Ecommerce Anti-Patterns

Reject:

* unclear product information
* weak product imagery
* hidden costs
* difficult checkout
* missing inventory states
* poor mobile purchasing

# Review Checklist

Before approval:

```text
Can users find products easily?

Are products easy to evaluate?

Is purchase confidence high?

Is checkout simple?

Are important states handled?

Does mobile shopping feel natural?

Is accessibility supported?
```
